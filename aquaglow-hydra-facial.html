Aquaglow Hydra Facial

Dull skin is visibly improved with this results-driven treatment. Completely painless, it clears away dead skin and congestion, floods the skin with antioxidants for hydration, and finishes with a medical-grade LED.

The treatment works in stages. A double cleanse comes first, then the hydradermabrasion handpiece uses an AHA solution to loosen and lift away dead skin and draw out congestion, then floods the skin with antioxidants for hydration. A medical-grade LED treatment and SPF finish it off. It minimises the appearance of fine lines, sun damage, blackheads and rough texture, and leaves the skin clean and glowing straight away.

Ideal for: dull skin, blackheads, congestion, fine lines, sun damage, rough texture, sensitive skin

Includes double cleanse, AHA peel solution, hydradermabrasion treatment, antioxidant serum infusion, LED treatment, SPF
