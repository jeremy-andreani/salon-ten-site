Your First Visit

Your First Visit

Haven’t been to Salon Ten before? Here is exactly what happens, from the moment you pull up outside to the moment you leave.

Getting here

We are at 5/7 Wilsons Road, Mount Hutton, in the row of shops known as Fords Corner leading up to Lake Macquarie Square. There is plenty of free parking at the front and back of the salon, and you can find us on the map on our contact page.

We suggest arriving at least 10 minutes before your appointment time, so you have a moment to unwind and enjoy a welcome drink.

When you arrive

You will be greeted by one of our friendly therapists. If the therapists are in treatment there is no need to ring the bell at the counter — we know you are coming. Just take a seat and start relaxing.

One of the therapists will give you a short form to fill in before your treatment begins.

Before you come in

Please come without makeup for a facial
We ask that you don’t wear makeup to a facial appointment, as we will only be taking it off.
No mascara for a lash lift or tint
Please don’t wear mascara if you are coming in for a lash lift or a tint.
Patch tests
A patch test is required before IPL. You can also request one if you are concerned about your skin reacting to an ingredient.
Nut allergies
Our massage oil is soy based, so it is nut free.
Anything to avoid beforehand
Some treatments need you to avoid or stop something in advance. Where that applies, we will have a consultation with you before your treatment to discuss it.

Aftercare and home care

Aftercare advice, or a treatment plan, is part of what we do. After a facial treatment we will also recommend home care to help you maintain your results between visits — that is part of our duty of care. It is always your choice.

Rebooking before you leave

We will ask whether you would like to rebook before you go. The salon is in high demand, so rebooking secures your preferred time — but it also keeps the results of your treatment going. Your therapist is trained to understand the growth cycles of skin, hair and nails, and it is much easier to stay on top of your treatment than to start all over again if you miss the recommended cycle.

Still have a question? Our FAQ covers parking, payment and changing an appointment, and our policies page covers cancellations and what we ask of you in the salon.

Ready to book?

Book online, or call the salon and one of the team will find a time that suits you.
