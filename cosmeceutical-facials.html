Cosmeceutical Facials

Designed for those looking for a more results-driven facial. Our cosmeceutical facials use medical-grade actives chosen for your skin on the day, working on texture, firmness, tone and clarity — with all the comfort of a proper salon facial. Choose the OptiGlow Thermal Lift when you want the corrective power of light, or the Hyperformance Medi-Facial for a treatment built around your skin on the day.

Resurface, renew and illuminate

OptiGlow Thermal Lift Facial $229

This advanced multi-stage treatment begins with a precision microdermabrasion or enzyme peel to sweep away dull skin cells and instantly refine texture. Once your skin is prepped, it’s treated to the corrective power of Broadband Rejuvenation (BBR) and targeted LED phototherapy. This gentle, cell-stimulating heat works deep within the tissue to remodel collagen and elastin and soften pigment, redness and fine lines — leaving your complexion visibly lifted, firmer and intensely radiant. While the light does its work you’ll have a hand or foot massage, so the treatment is as restful as it is corrective. We finish with a Vitamin A peel.

Includes microdermabrasion or enzyme peel, Broadband Rejuvenation, LED phototherapy, hand or foot massage, Vitamin A peel

Kellie will consult with you on whether this treatment is right for your skin.

Works powerfully on its own, and the ideal way to maintain your results after a course of BBR & IPL Skin Rejuvenation. If you’d like to build on what you see, Kellie can plan a course around your skin.

No two skins. No two facials.

Hyperformance Medi-Facial $184

Designed on the day, for the skin you arrive with. There is no set protocol. Your skin is assessed and your therapist chooses from a range of modalities, building the treatment around what your skin actually needs. Skin changes with the season, your hormones, your stress levels and whatever you’ve been using at home; a treatment chosen weeks ago when you booked can’t account for any of that. This one is decided once we’ve looked at your skin, not before.

Your treatment may include some of the following: microdermabrasion, a skin peel, plasma infusion, nano infusion, LED, cosmeceutical serum masks, and a hand, foot or signature scalp, neck and shoulder massage — chosen on the day to suit your skin and the time your treatment takes.

Ideal for: skin that changes with the season or your hormones, a first treatment with us, and keeping your skin in good order between corrective treatments
