Reiki

Many people come in curious and leave surprised by how deeply relaxed and calm they feel. When you’re stressed, anxious or rundown your body can get stuck in fight or flight mode. Reiki can help shift you out of that and into a deep and peaceful state of calm and relaxation.

Reiki Body Reset with Carlissa $175

You stay fully clothed and lie comfortably on the treatment bed. This isn’t a massage treatment, though light, appropriate touch can be included — Carlissa rests her hands on or just above the body and works through a sequence of positions while you rest, with essential oils through the treatment. Most people drift somewhere between awake and asleep.

Carlissa has completed Certificates 1 and 2 in Reiki healing, and brings the same calm, unhurried presence her clients know her for.

What people tell us they feel: a profound sense of calm and relaxation, reduced anxiety and mental noise, better sleep in the days that follow, and a feeling of being “reset” — lighter and more themselves.
