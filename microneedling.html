Skin Microneedling

Skin Needling from $329

Also known as collagen induction therapy

Skin needling is a clinically proven, medical-grade treatment effective in addressing a wide range of skin concerns, including aging, acne scarring, open pores, pigmentation, broken capillaries, and sun damage. The treatment uses fine, sterile needles to create controlled micro-injury in the skin, stimulating the body’s natural wound-healing response and triggering collagen and elastin production. With each session, skin is strengthened and rebuilt from within, resulting in a stronger, healthier, and more youthful and radiant complexion over time.

Why have your treatment with us? Skin needling isn’t really about the device — it’s about knowing how your skin will respond to it. Kellie has been working with skin since 2003 and is studying an Associate Degree in Applied Health Science (Dermal Therapy), so your treatment is planned around what’s actually driving your concern and how your skin heals, not a standard setting applied to every face. You’ll know what to expect at each stage of recovery before you book.

Before your first treatment, Kellie will do a quick assessment, either in the salon or over the phone, to check that skin needling is the right choice for you — some skin conditions are better suited to a different treatment, and she’ll talk you through the options if that’s the case. Your appointment then includes a consultation to discuss your goals and agree a treatment plan.

Ideal for: preventative anti-ageing, acne scarring, open pores, broken capillaries, sun damage, skin maintenance, texture and tone improvement
