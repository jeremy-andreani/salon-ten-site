Skin Microneedling

Skin Needling from $329

Also known as collagen induction therapy

60 minutes (including consultation)

Skin needling is a clinically proven, medical-grade treatment effective in addressing a wide range of skin concerns, including aging, acne scarring, open pores, pigmentation, broken capillaries, and sun damage. The treatment uses fine, sterile needles to create controlled micro-injury in the skin, stimulating the body’s natural wound-healing response and triggering collagen and elastin production. With each session, skin is strengthened and rebuilt from within, resulting in a stronger, healthier, and more youthful and radiant complexion over time.

A consultation is included prior to treatment to assess suitability, discuss your goals, and determine an appropriate treatment plan.

Ideal for: preventative anti-aging, acne scarring, open pores, broken capillaries, sun damage, skin maintenance, texture and tone improvement

Includes consultation, prep, skin needling treatment, post-care
