LED Skin Treatments from $59

LED is a clinically proven, non-invasive & soothing skin treatment that immediately makes your skin feel tighter & appear more alive due to increased circulation, cellular activity & moisture. Our red LED promotes collagen and elastin production so you will notice a healthy glow & skin improvement with no down time. Our blue LED is excellent for treating acne. Our infrared LED is perfect for healing the skin and skin rejuvenation at a deeper level.

We recommend a course of treatments if you are wanting to treat specific skin concerns. However the treatment can be used as a stand alone treatment for an instant radiant glow making it perfect for a special occasion or if you are feeling like your skin needs an instant pick-me-up!

Radiance Reboot Facial $145

50 minutes

Give your skin a fresh start with our Radiance Reboot Facial — designed to brighten, firm, and restore that healthy glow.
