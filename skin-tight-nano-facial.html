Nano Infusion Facial

Our Nano Infusion Facial utilises the Rezenerate skin wand, a revolutionary, non-invasive and pain-free treatment that restores a more youthful radiance to your skin’s appearance using state-of-the-art nano-technology. Beginning with a double cleanse and exfoliation to prepare the skin, followed by the Rezenerate nano treatment, which gently separates dead cells from the skin’s surface while simultaneously infusing applied antioxidants directly into the skin. This dual action boosts hydration and delivers active ingredients where they’re needed most, immediately leaving skin more hydrated, smoother, and tighter-looking, with cell repair and renewal activated from within. We finish with SPF to protect your refreshed skin. Great as a pick-me-up or in the week leading up to a big event, leaving skin firmer, more radiant, and camera-ready with zero downtime.

Ideal for: dull, tired, or lax skin, a hydration and radiance boost, pre-event, wedding prep
