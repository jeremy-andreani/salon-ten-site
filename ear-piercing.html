Ear Piercing

Ear Piercing $95

For ages 5+. All ear piercings include sterile studs and our after-care antiseptic “magic spray”.

Your piercing is done by Kellie, who has over 24 years’ experience piercing ears, in a clean, private and comfortable space. You and your child are involved in the process, with each step explained as we go, and the guns we use are extremely quiet so there’s nothing to startle them. Our piercing studs come in a choice of birthstones, in both silver and gold. If you would like a particular colour we recommend letting us know in advance as we will endeavour to have your preference in stock.
