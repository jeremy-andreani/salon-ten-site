Ear Piercing $95

For ages 3+. All ear piercings include earrings and after-care antiseptic spray.

At SALON TEN we are highly experienced in ear piercing offering a clean, private and comfortable environment using two therapists, (if available), to pierce your ears at the same time to minimise fuss and get the job done quickly. Our therapists are great with the children involving them in the process and making it a pleasant experience for them, using guns that are extremely quiet and do not startle. Our aftercare solution “magic spray” is the newest on the market and reduces the healing time from 6 weeks down to 3. We offer studs in a range of birthstones in both gold and silver. If you would like a particular colour we recommend letting us know in advance as we will endeavour to have your preference in stock.
