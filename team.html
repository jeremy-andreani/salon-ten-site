Our Team

Meet the Team

Kellie

Skin Therapist & Owner

Hi, I am Kellie, owner and skin therapist here at SALON TEN. I started my career in 2003 after graduating and since then have invested my knowledge and love for helping other people into SALON TEN.

I thrive on continuing my knowledge and education with undergraduate studies in nutrition, gut health, dermal science (the skin and it’s many conditions) and cosmetic chemistry. I love and enjoy training the therapists at SALON TEN so that my team and I can deliver the best possible treatments and customer experience.

If you’re looking for a solution to a skin issue, don’t know where to start and want help to understand what’s going on and how you can resolve it, come and see me for an interesting, fun and comprehensive skin assessment that will have you feeling back in control and confident. Don’t get caught up in the marketing hype. The team and I at SALON TEN look forward to seeing you soon.

Monika

Beauty Therapist

Meet Monika, our Diploma-qualified, talented and passionate beauty therapist. Monika’s dedication to providing exceptional service is reflected in her warm and caring approach to every client she meets. She thrives on helping clients feel relaxed, rejuvenated, and fabulous, ensuring that each experience is tailored to their unique needs.

Monika’s expertise spans a range of beauty treatments, from facials and massages to waxing and skincare, always with a focus on relaxation to make you look and feel your absolute best.

Outside of her professional life, Monika loves spending time outdoors, whether it’s hiking, exploring new destinations, or simply soaking up nature. When she’s not out exploring, Monika enjoys indulging her creative side with arts and crafts.

Monika is available Mondays, Thursdays and Fridays and is looking forward to meeting you!

Carlissa

Beauty Therapist

Meet Carlissa, diploma qualified with an impressive 22 years’ experience in the beauty industry, Carlissa brings a wealth of knowledge, skill and passion to every treatment she performs.

A true perfectionist by nature and an excellent all-rounder therapist, Carlissa excels in all areas of beauty therapy but especially loves nurturing her clients with relaxing facials. Her calming presence and meticulous attention to detail will have you feeling pampered and cared for.

Outside of her professional life, Carlissa finds balance through yoga, reading and spending time at the beach. She cherishes quality time with her daughter and their much-loved dog, Teddy.

Carlissa looks forward to welcoming you and is available Mondays, Tuesdays and Wednesdays to help you look and feel your absolute best, inside and out!
