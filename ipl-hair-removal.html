IPL Hair Removal

IPL (Intense Pulsed Light) hair removal uses targeted pulses of light to reduce hair growth, on areas from the lip through to full legs.

IPL and SHR in one. Our SkinMed Transcend is a TGA-listed Australian machine that works two ways, and your therapist picks whichever suits your skin and hair. Traditional IPL uses single, targeted flashes. SHR (Super Hair Removal) glides over the area with rapid, gentler pulses — not painful, just a warm sensation as the handpiece moves.

It’s the same machine behind our BBR & IPL Skin Rejuvenation treatments.
