Clinical Skin Peels

A results-driven facial. Our cosmeceutical peels are progressive rather than aggressive — they work with your skin to rejuvenate, strengthen and increase circulation, improving texture and elasticity. Whether you want a light peel to refresh and hydrate, a deeper peel for a more stubborn concern, or a regular maintenance schedule to keep your skin healthy, your therapist will work out the right peel and home care plan at your consultation.

Reveal Medi-Peel from $129

Our peels are medical grade and tailored to your skin on the day. While the peel is working, you’ll have a neck and shoulder massage — so you get the relaxation as well as the result. A course of 4–6 peels is recommended for the best long-lasting results.

Ideal for: acne, congestion, sun damage, pigmentation, dehydration, fine lines, sensitive skin
