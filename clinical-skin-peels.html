Clinical Skin Peels

Designed for those looking for a more results driven facial. Our cosmeceutical ingredients and peels support the skin in rejuvenation, whilst strengthening and increasing circulation. They improve skin texture and elasticity, can heal acne and congestion, lighten discoloration, repair sun damage, help to hydrate and soften lines and wrinkles. Whether a light peel to refresh and hydrate the skin is needed or a deeper peel for more stubborn skin conditions, our peels are affordable, effective and will suit most skin types and conditions. Through a thorough consultation with your therapist the right peel and home care treatment plan can be determined for your skin.
