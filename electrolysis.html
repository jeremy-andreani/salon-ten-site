Electrolysis

Placeholder image

from $37

Electrolysis is a safe method of permanent hair removal. A very fine probe is used to treat each hair follicle individually with a tiny electrical current, which stops that follicle producing new hair.

Because it works one follicle at a time, electrolysis suits hair that other methods can struggle with, including fine, fair and grey hair, and it is well suited to small areas such as the chin, upper lip, brows and the odd stray hair. It is suitable for all skin tones and hair types.

Hair grows in cycles, so electrolysis works best as a series of appointments over time rather than a single visit. Your therapist will talk you through what to expect for your own hair growth at your first appointment.

Choosing an appointment length: 10 minutes suits a few hairs, or returning clients. 20 minutes suits a first visit, or where there is more hair to treat.
