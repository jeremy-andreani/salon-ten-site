Electrolysis

Electrolysis is a safe method of permanent hair removal. A very fine probe is used to treat each hair follicle individually with a tiny electrical current, which stops that follicle producing new hair.

Because it works one follicle at a time, electrolysis suits hair that other methods can struggle with, including fine, fair and grey hair, and it is well suited to areas such as the chin, upper lip, neck and the odd stray hair. It is suitable for all skin tones and hair types.

Hair grows in cycles, so electrolysis works best as a series of appointments over time rather than a single visit.
