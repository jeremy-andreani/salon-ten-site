Dull, lifeless skin is visibly improved with this results-oriented treatment. It’s completely painless & perfect to minimise the appearance of fine lines, unwanted pigmentation, sun-damage, enlarged pores, blackheads & rough skin texture.

Microdermabrasion Facial $189

60 minutes

Reveal smoother, brighter skin with our signature Microdermabrasion Facial — a comprehensive treatment designed to deeply refresh and rejuvenate. We begin with a double cleanse to fully prepare the skin, followed by microdermabrasion to gently exfoliate and resurface, buffing away dull, dead skin cells. An antioxidant serum and mask work together to nourish and replenish, while a relaxing hand or foot treatment lets you unwind fully. LED therapy is then used to target specific skin concerns and boost your results, and we finish with SPF to protect your newly revealed glow. The perfect treatment for anyone wanting a complete reset — inside and out.

Ideal for: dull, congested, or uneven skin

Includes double cleanse, microdermabrasion, antioxidant serum & mask, hand or foot treatment, LED therapy, SPF

30 minutes

Short on time but still want that fresh-faced glow? Our Microdermabrasion Lite treatment delivers a quick, effective refresh. A great option for regular skin maintenance or when you need a fast pick-me-up before a big event.
