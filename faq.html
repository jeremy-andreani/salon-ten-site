FAQ & Salon Policies

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there parking?
Free parking at the front and back of the salon.
When should I arrive?
10 minutes before your appointment.
What payment do you accept?
Cash, EFTPOS and major credit cards (no AMEX or cheques).
Do you have a referral program?
Yes – refer a friend and receive $20 off your next treatment.
What are your gift voucher terms?
Vouchers are purchased and redeemed through Kitomba.

Salon Policies

  • Cancellations: 24 hours’ notice required, or 50% of the treatment value applies.
  • No-shows: full treatment fee applies, plus a 50% deposit on future bookings.
  • Late arrival: shortens your treatment time; full price still applies.
  • Children are not able to be brought into the salon, to keep the space relaxing for all guests.
  • Group bookings require a non-refundable 50% deposit.
