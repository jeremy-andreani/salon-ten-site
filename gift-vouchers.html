Gift Vouchers

A Salon Ten gift voucher is the one thing you can never have enough of. It can be put towards any treatment or any product in the salon, and it is valid for three years from the day it is bought — so there is no rush to use it.

A voucher suits the occasions when you want to give someone something they would not book for themselves.

Ideal for: birthdays, Mother’s Day, Christmas, anniversaries, a thank you, the bridal party, a new mum, teacher gifts, or anyone you have run out of ideas for.
